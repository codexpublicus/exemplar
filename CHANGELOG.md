# Changelog

Changes to the specs, schemas, vectors and examples, by release tag. A release tag pins the court record key spec (CRK), the Akoma Ntoso profile and the court registry together; each spec's own versioning section (CRK section 13, profile section 18) says what its versions mean. Earlier versions are described there.

## v0.4.0

CRK 0.4.0, profile 0.4.0, court registry `2026.10.23`, and a new CAP court table, `registry/cap-courts.json` (`2026.10.23`). Minor versions that break, under SemVer item 4. The jurisdiction codes are the owner's rulings D101 (2026-10-23) and D104 (2026-10-24); the registry additions and the CAP court table D105 (2026-10-24); and the key model, dates included, D102 (2026-10-25), with D113 (2026-10-25) on what makes a key provisional, which the `scotus-numbered` profile for the pre-1971 Supreme Court (D103) follows. The dates and the Supreme Court close two of the gaps of [exemplar#46](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/46).

### Court record key 0.4.0

- **Jurisdiction codes** ([exemplar#25](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/25), [#38](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/38), [#39](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/39), [#43](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/43); D101, D104). Section 3 is rewritten: the first segment, now named the jurisdiction segment, is a jurisdiction code, an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code, a territory's own code (`pr`, `gu`, `vi`, `as`, `mp`), a registered body code of three to sixteen letters (`ccj`), or the issuing sovereign's code for a court whose sovereign changed: `england` before 1707-05-01 and `gb` from it, for English and colonial courts alike, and `us` from 1776-07-04 for the colonies. A court outside the United States takes the code of the country it sits in; federal courts seated in a territory stay `us`. This version defines `us`, `pr`, `gu`, `vi`, `as`, `mp`, `gb`, `ph`, `ccj`, `england`, `hawaii`, `texas` and `vermont`. The ABNF's `jurisdiction` is `2*16LC`, and a validity rule admits the listed codes. The error class `country` is renamed `jurisdiction`, and a parsed key's part `country` is `jurisdiction` (D104(f)). Tribal courts stay under `us` ([exemplar#45](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/45)). **Changes keys**: in CAP, 44,295 Puerto Rico, 2,135 American Samoa, 1,708 Virgin Islands, 158 Guam and 110 Northern Mariana Islands keys move from `us` to the territory's code; 720 colonial keys move to `gb`, 13 to `england`, and 1,168 Hawaiian Kingdom keys to `hawaii` (50,307 in all).
- **Dates** ([exemplar#37](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/37), [#33](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/33); D102). Section 6 is rewritten: `decided` is the most precise date a producer can establish from trustworthy sources at minting, never padded, with the day, or the month and the day, zeroed where it can't establish them (`1882-05-00`, `1793-00-00`). The segment stays ten characters wide; it is not ISO 8601, and stands for the first day of its period wherever a calendar date is needed. A date established later mints a new key, and the old one is `replaced-by` it. CourtListener's approximate dates are established to the year when they fall on 1 July and to the month otherwise. The ABNF's `decided` is `date`, and the regex's date group is `[0-9]{4}-[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}`. The refusal class `incomplete-date` is withdrawn. **Adds keys**.
- **Printed-date alias** (D102). New section 6.3 and alias scheme `crk-printed`: the decision key computed from the decision's printing in its official report alone, with the printed date zeroed and the ordinal in printed order, so anyone holding the volume can compute it. It resolves to the decision's current key.
- **The key model** (D102). Section 13 gains the address guarantee (13.1: a minted key always resolves to its decision, a superseded key is a permanent `replaced-by` redirect, a retired key is never reused), the minted-key ledger (13.2: the key computation yields a candidate the ledger confirms or advances, so ordinals are frozen at minting and later arrivals take the next free ordinal), provisional keys (13.3: a ledger mark, not part of the key, where trustworthy sources disagree; a date the official report prints in full is made provisional only by an official or human-curated source, never by CourtListener's dates, engine output or other padded or estimated data, D113, 2026-10-25) and errata (13.4: an outright error is corrected only through the publisher's published, append-only errata list, `schema/crk-errata.schema.json`). Section 12 computes a candidate; the old "freeze at mint" paragraph is folded into 13.1, and the versioning text is section 13.5. **Changes keys**: a minted key no longer follows a later change in its neighbours' facts.
- **Supreme Court before 1971-07-01** (D103, following D102). New section 5.7, profile `scotus-numbered`: the appellate number, `-misc` for the Miscellaneous docket and `-orig` for the Original docket (`us/scotus/13-orig/1904-12-12`), with the initial decision ordinal in printed order, frozen at minting; other decisions of the period take the reporter fallback. **Adds keys**.
- **Courts** ([exemplar#26](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/26), [#27](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/27); D105). Sections 4 and 10.1: a source's court id is translated through registry aliases (`courtlistener:txctapp5` is `texapp`) or the CAP court table. New refusal class `court-under-review` (section 12): a CAP court the table marks for review gives no key until a person checks it. Section 16 drops the pre-1971 Supreme Court and adds the questions this version leaves open.

- CAP's and CourtListener's date mappings (sections 15.1, 15.3) follow CRK 0.4.0 section 6. Open question O7 narrows to what still has no key.

## v0.3.0 (unreleased)

### Court record key 0.3.0

- **Docket cleaning** ([exemplar#12](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/12)). Section 5.1 is replaced: label removal ignores letter case and covers `Appeal No.`, `Appeal, No.`, `Docket No.`, `File No.`, `Gen. No.` and the like; a thousands separator is removed (`31,079` gives `31079`); a list of dockets (`;`, or `Nos.` with `,`, `&` or `and`) is keyed by its first, and the others are aliases; a docket with no digit is no docket. Section 5.5 gains a step: a comma between digits becomes `_` (`131, 1997` gives `131_1997`) and every other comma is deleted. **Changes keys**: the same printed docket can give a different segment than under 0.2.0, and a decision that fell back to `rpt.` can now have a docket key.
- **Fallback ordinal** ([exemplar#13](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/13)). Section 5.6: a fallback decision's ordinal is its position among every decision that begins on the same reporter page and shares its date, whether or not they take the fallback; section 7 points to it. Glover, 500 Pa. 524, is `us/pa/rpt.500-pa-524/1983-04-27~2`. **Decides keys** 0.2.0 left ambiguous.
- **Printed forms in a judge registry** ([exemplar#14](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/14) part b). Section 10.2: an entry MAY list the strings in which a source prints the judge's name, OCR errors included, each with its source; a producer matches a byline only to a name or printed form, exactly, never by approximation. Adds keys for parts 0.2.0 refused; changes none.

### Akoma Ntoso profile 0.3.0

- **Per curiam bylines** ([exemplar#14](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/14) part a). Section 15.4: a `majority` byline that reduces to `percuriam`, `bythecourt` or `thecourt` is `per-curiam`. **Changes part keys**: an unsigned `By the Court.` or `THE COURT.` opinion goes from `.../opinion` to `.../per-curiam`.
- **Titles above a byline** ([exemplar#15](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/15)). Section 7.2: paragraphs printed before a byline are `span`s in the part's `heading`, numbered with its paragraphs, and the byline moves to `subheading`. New rule AKN-59; AKN-34, AKN-35 and AKN-40 follow.
- **Reporter lines inside an opinion** ([exemplar#16](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/16)). New section 8.4 and a change to 12.1: a paragraph the source classes as head matter inside a writing keeps its place and carries `@class` and a `@cx:origin` other than `court`. New rule AKN-60.
- **Head matter classes** ([exemplar#17](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/17)). Section 6 adds the class `citation` and allows `courtType` only on text that names the court, with `@cx:provenance="engine-output"`; section 15.2 maps `p.citation` and restricts `p.court`.
- **Page markers** ([exemplar#18](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/18); proposed as 0.2.1, folded in). Section 9.1 says how `@cx:scan` is read from `data-blocks`, never from the page offset, and that `@breakAt` and `@breakWith` are absent without source data; section 16.3 says which of several entries for a page a consumer uses.
- **Printing facts from volume records** ([exemplar#19](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/19)). Section 5.3: `@ordinal` is required when another decision begins on the page, determined from the source's volume records; `@text` gains `released`, and every value but `transcribed` is `engine-output`. AKN-24 checks both values and provenance; the sidecar schema's `text` gains `released`.
- **Citation links** ([exemplar#20](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/20)). Section 15.2: a CAP citation link is a decision when it has `data-case-paths`, or when its `cites_to` entry, matched with whitespace removed, has a `reporters:` category; the aliases are formed from `data-cite`.
- **Footnote markers** ([exemplar#21](https://github.com/codexpublicus/exemplar/issues/21); proposed as 0.2.1, folded in). Sections 11.1 and 11.2: `@marker` is the note's label as the source gives it, OCR errors included, never corrected.
